Lacuna

Sacha Syntax, 2025

Lacuna is a post-digital noise work by Sacha Syntax. The outskirts of the digital soundscape are here explored: imploded sonic fetishes, marginal acoustic phenomena, incomprehensible regurgitations of the semiogenetic process. The symbolic construction of a-symbolic structures: a construction that somehow feigns its own imminent collapse. Each track is the grotesque yet unsettling ghost of a sonic panorama that is difficult to reconstruct: and whose past or postulated existence can only be intuited.

Simulation of Discrete Phase Unit Cells in Acoustic Meta-Holography

sacha syntax, 2025

This study investigates acoustic unit cells arranged on metasurfaces. By controlling the local properties of each cell, complex multi-focal pressure patterns emerge across the image plane. Simulations indicate that discrete acoustic elements can shape and manipulate wavefronts, producing controllable and reproducible acoustic phenomena.
